6. **Secondary actions:** Open goal Details, Full Stats, the hint, team info, performance info, Reset or My Agendas on the calendar, Skip or Quick, rail navigation.
7. **Layout hierarchy:** Left half: header stack top-left (turn chip, date strip, Unity Cup chip), goal banner right of it, Energy and mood below, team laurel on the left edge, hint bubble top-centre, trainee art centre with the Silver chip and Full Stats button floating over it, stat band mid-lower, then two rows of action buttons (three plus three), Skip and Quick along the bottom. Right half: Scheduled Races card with tabs, grid, footer. Nav rail far right.
8. **Visual hierarchy:** The six action buttons first (large, colour-coded, centred under the stat band). The stat band second. The goal banner third. The calendar is equal in area but visually quieter: white cells, small race cards. The hint bubble draws by position (speech bubble at top-centre) rather than colour.
9. **What carries state:** Energy is bar length plus gradient colour; mood is a colour word pill with arrow; team rank is a laurel letter with a tag word; performance tier is the Silver chip; stats are value/denominator/grade; available actions are encoded by button colour and enabled styling (Infirmary greyed); the current half-month is the yellow cell; goals carry red "Goal" pennants and already-scheduled races carry pink "Scheduled" pills; entry readiness is the orange "Entry criteria met!" sub-line.
10. **Absences:** No turn number within the year beyond the date strip; no stat gain preview for the highlighted action; no confirmation for Rest or Recreation; the calendar cells show race names but no grades or distances until opened; no mood detail beyond the single word.
11. **What this screen teaches the app:** The turn is a six-way choice presented flat, and the game nudges the choice with a contextual hint that names the weak stat, so guidance lives on the decision surface rather than behind a help system.
