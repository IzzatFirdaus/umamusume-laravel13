Unity Cup or Trackblazer Scenario?

We can expect the updated Unity Cup to be the go-to scenario for building strong, competitive aces temporarily until the release of Grand Live. Trackblazer will remain still as a viable parent-building scenario choice.

The Unity Cup update has powercrept PvP Trackblazer training fairly quickly. In the updated Unity Cup:

  • You can build consistent 17,000+ rating veterans with 2,000-2,500+ Skill Points (Throne Group SSR considered), fairly close still to Trackblazer's average of 2,800.
  • Get a large amount of stats out of training, still with only minimal race wins (around 11-15).
  • With the new stat caps, get UG-grade stat lines of at most or close to 1300.
  • Get free training turns with giant stat gains now thanks to frequent Extreme Spirit Burst availability, similar to using Training Weights and other item buffs with a Good-Luck Charm in Trackblazer.
  • The original suggested deck setups for Unity Cup are still good, so you can now improve them with new cards without much dependency on Race Bonus cards.

This is on top of other benefits for scenarios that use the standard career goal system outside of Trackblazer: you can take New Year dialogue choices again, get the hot spring lottery buffs, and take trainee secret events.

The Stat Cap Has Increased

1300 1300 1300 1300 1800

The Unity Cup Scenario now has +100 stat caps for Speed, Stamina, Power, and Guts, with a +600 stat cap for Wit. When a stat's cap is about to be increased, the values that will be added will always be indicated in gold text.

  • Training gains for stats beyond 1200 are always halved.
  • Stat caps are obtained mainly from sparks, inspiration, specific support events, and gains beyond the original base 1200 cap, whether through training or other means.

Though training past stat caps can take quite an effort due to fewer training gains, there's an advantage to having beyond 1200 stats, noticeably with the mechanic Fully Charged being introduced early and requiring a power stat of 1200 or more.

Cap increases give you more room to create even better Veterans for PvP modes such as Champions Meetings and Team Trials.

Stat Cap Increase Guide

Extreme Spirit Bursts Available

Extreme Spirit Bursts (ESB) can now appear as of the July 1, 2026 update. These appear as the purple versions of Spirit Bursts. Compared to regular Spirit Bursts:

  • ESB gives Ignited Spirit skills and significantly bigger stat boosts.
  • ESB can appear on a Support that has already activated her regular Spirit Burst.
  • ESB can disappear and reappear when left unused, just like regular Spirit Bursts.
  • There is a 0% Failure Rate in the training facility where the Support has active ESB.
  • ESB, after activating once, no longer repeats on the same Support (this will show a purple flame symbol on their icon).

Given the above, it is highly recommended to seek out Extreme Spirit Bursts since they're technically free training and give an enormous amount of stats, comparable to stacking Training Weights, Megaphones, and a Good-Luck Charm in Trackblazer (MANT).

You will also need to activate Extreme Spirit Burst at least once as part of the conditions of encountering the Elite Team on the 4th round of the Unity Cup.

Extra It's On Rewarded on Team Rank S+

It's On! Increase velocity when passing another runner mid-race.

On top of reaching overall team rank S for the Unity Cup for It's On, you will also get an additional +1 Hint Level for it when you reach team rank S+. Additional Hint Levels are added if your trainee is scenario-linked (you're training Taiki Shuttle, for example).

Elite Team and S+ Team Zenith

Elite Team Unlock Conditions

Elite Team Unlock Conditions
・Your Team rating is 10 or higher.
・Your Overall Team rank is A or higher.
・You have activated Extreme Spirit Burst at least once.
Elite Team Reward Sample

A stronger opposing Team in the 4th round of Unity Cup, Elite Team, can appear when you satisfy the above conditions. They will appear as a Rank 2 team with a pink background in the team selection menu and are named after Greek figures (Team Aeon or Team Hephaestus, for example). Winning against this team in a Team Race will grant large boosts to all stats (around 20+ for each stat).

S+ Team Zenith Unlock Conditions

S+ Team Zenith Rewards Sample

S+ Team Zenith can be encountered when you beat the Elite Team in the 4th Unity Cup round. Defeating this team also grants greater stat boosts (around 28 for each stat, for example) and has a chance to give Unity Cup plus (+) sparks (for example, Ignited Spirit: Speed +).

Unity Cup Plus Sparks

Defeating S+ Team Zenith gives your trainee a chance to roll an Ignited Spirit plus spark. These sparks provide both a stat increase and a skill hint for the Ignited Spirit skill when they are triggered during an inspiration event.

Note that, like other spark, this is spark is not completely guaranteed. If you're aiming for these sparks, you can use the reroll feature at the end of the run (note that this costs 30 TP).

How to Reroll Sparks

Clocks Usable for Failed Unity Cup Races

Should you lose any of the Unity Cup round Team races, you can now use clocks to redo them. This makes dealing with Unity Cup races less tedious, since previously 1 lost Unity Cup race can be overall disastrous for building a strong veteran.

・Focus on Unity Training with your teammates to win Team Races and get bonus stats in career.

・Training Facility levels are raised through raising team stat ranks.

Train with Teammates to Win Team Races

Unity Cup Team Race Schedules
Round 1 After Junior Year Late Dec
Round 2 After Classic Year Late June
Round 3 After Classic Year Late Dec
Round 4 After Senior Year Late June
Finals After Senior Year Late Dec

Every six months in your career (Late June and Late December), you will have to participate in Team Races which has you fielding five teams of racers similar to Team Trials.

In each Team Race is a set of five races covering each distance (Sprint, Mile, Medium, Long, Dirt). Once you have completed all five races, you will receive an increase to all stats, with the amount based on your performance.

For the best result of your trainee's career, make sure to win these team races. The difficulty of team races can be overcome by taking advantage of the Spirit Burst Mechanic to raise team member stats and organizing your team members according to their preferred aptitudes.

How to Win Unity Cup Team Races

No Need to Maximize Non-Career Races

In the Unity Cup, it is preferrable to maximize your teammate training (developing Spirit Bursts) over taking regular races unless these races contribute to your race goals, since taking a race means you'll be taking a turn off from developing your teammates' stats and rank, directly affecting also your progress of raising your Training Facility levels.

Maximize Teammate Training with Wit Cards

Recommended SSR Wit Cards
Fine Motion (Wave of Gratitude) Nice Nature (Daring to Dream) Mejiro Dober (My Thoughts, My Desires)

In Unity Cup, maximizing your Training turns is recommended to also increase training sessions with teammates. Wit Support Cards have great value in this case, since they increase energy recovery in the Wit facility on top of also being a teammate training node. Having energy means you don't have to resort too much to resting.

Having 2-3 Wit cards will be a viable setup in the Unity Cup, even in PvP. This is typically good for Sprint-Mile setups, but you can adjust for just 1 Wit card for more Stamina and Guts options for longer distance builds.

You're free to use any other SR card, such as Marvelous Sunday Wit SR or Matikane Fukukitaru Wit SR, if you don't have the above SSR Wit cards.

Set Up Unity Cup Team Members

Organizing Team Members
・Team members can be arranged between distances, similar to Team Trials. Place them where their aptitudes are at A for the best results.
・A minimum of 1 racer is required for each distance. Up to 3 racers can be placed in a distance team.
・Filling a team with higher ranks is recommended.

Your team members will consist of your Trainee, your Support Cards, and other characters throughout the career. Characters besides those in your Support Card deck can join at random.

Characters' stats and ranks are raised by training with them, and their ranks increasing will contribute to the increase of your overall team rank.

How to Increase Team Rank (Unity Cup)

Race Goals Are Still The Same

Although the Unity Cup scenario revolves around the Unity Cup races, you will still need to do well in your trainee's race goals, including the final 3 URA Finale races. Similar to the URA Finale, failing your trainee's race goals enough times will lead to your career run ending.

Aim For Rank 5 Against Team Zenith

Team Zenith can be difficult to beat if your Team Rank is not high enough. This is because your rank also determines the bonus to all attributes, with the highest being +50 at rank 5 or above. This makes it possible for a team with an A rating to beat them. To ensure you can get to Rank 5, you must win the middle option once (except during Classic Year, Late December, or 1,1,2,1) and win the top option for all the other race periods.

Usually, picking the middle option for the 1st race is the safest bet (2,1,1,1). Alternatively, if you manage to reach the 4th race after winning the top race everytime, you can safely pick the middle option (1,1,1,2) and not lose out on much. You also avoid the risk of losing against the Turf Queens or The Apex.

On each increase of your overall team rank (or Team Power), you will gain some overall stat bonuses. Overall team rank is raised naturally as you level enough overall team stats, so make sure to do as much Unity Training as you can!

Spirit Bursts and Unity Training Bonuses

Spirit Burst Mechanics
Increases the stat gains from a training facility when triggered.
・2 or more Spirit Bursts stack additively.
・Spirit Bursts can appear together with Friendship Training.
・Spirit Bursts, if not taken, can reappear on the same or another facility.
・Spirit Bursts increase training energy consumption with the exception of Wit, which increases energy gain instead.
・Only 1 Spirit Burst will be available per character. Once triggered, they will not appear again. A small blue flame indicator appears at the bottom-right of a character's icon once a Spirit Burst has been triggerd for them.
・Skill hint indicators will mostly be hidden due to the Unity Training indicators taking their place on the training icons, so you'll want to inspect every facility just to be sure if there are any skill hints.

While training, a flame icon will be present besides a teammate. Training on a facility with these teammates will increase their Spirit Burst Meter. When the Spirit Burst Meter is full, selecting a training with said teammate will trigger a Spirit Burst.

A Spirit Burst will give your trainee extra stats from the facility she trains on. This applies not just to your trainee, but also to the team member who triggered the burst. You will also a random skill hint whenever a Spirit Burst is triggered.

It is important to note that you do not need to immediately trigger a Spirit Burst. You can wait until the team member with a Spirit Burst is at the stat you want to increase before activating it.

The stats gained through Unity Training increases depending on the number of people involved in Unity Training. Unity Training with multiple Umas at once in a stat is the most efficient way to gain stats along with building their Spirit Burst meter.

Unity Training bonuses adds to Spirit Bursts. When Spirit Bursts happen, the increase from multiple participating Umas still occurs. For example, if you were to get +15 Stamina from a Spirit Burst of 1 Uma, the +2 from another Uma with Unity Training (total 2 Umas in training) will also apply.

ⓘ Rare versions of the skill have the "Burning" label. Common versions of them are referred to as "Ignited".

Completing several Spirit Bursts will reward a skill in Senior Year Early November. It is recommend that you maximize Spirit Bursts as much as you can to get the most amount of Hint Levels for the skill you will get!

The type of exclusive skill rewarded will depend on the highest stat rank of your team. If 2 or more team stat ranks are equal, a random exclusive skill will be given corresponding to 1 of those stat ranks.

Focus on Spirit Bursts Early On

It is recommended to prioritize Unity Training early on to raise the stats and rank of your team member, ensuring security of winning team races. Friendship Training bonds can come second, since Unity Training and Spirit Bursts do provide signifcant stat gains. But you'll want to make the most out of both the further you are in your career.

Spirit Burst Rewarded Skills

Skill Effect
Burning Spirit SPD / Ignited Spirit SPD Increases velocity mid-race. Scales according to the total Speed of team members.
Burning Spirit STA / Ignited Spirit STA Recovers endurance mid-race. Scales according to the total Stamina of team members.
Burning Spirit PWR / Ignited Spirit PWR Increases acceleration late-race. Scales according to the total Power of team members.
Burning Spirit GUTS / Ignited Spirit GUTS Increases velocity and acceleration late-race. Scales according to the total Guts of team members.
Burning Spirit WIT / Ignited Spirit WIT Improves navigation early-race. Scales according to the total Wit of team members.

Of the exclusive skills above, PWR looks to be the most beneficial for its late-race acceleration bonus. GUTS comes in second for providing velocity and acceleration in the late-race, and then SPD for mid-race velocity instead if you do not have either. STA is to be considered depending on your Stamina and recovery skill needs, and WIT is considered last since it just improves navigation in the early-race.

Note that the exclusive skills depend on your overall team's stat corresponding to that skill. For example, Burning Spirit PWR will have a stronger effect if each member of your team has high power stat. This means it'll work best in a triple Ace team well-developed with Power, and may be weaker in teams where 1 or more members are more developed in other stats (such as Wit for debuffing).

Check Spirit Bursts in Team Info

You can check how many Spirit Bursts you've done by clicking on the button beside your Team Rank. This will display your Team Info, which contains the total number of Unity Trainings and Spirit Bursts done so far.

Balance Adjustments (Nov. 11, 2025)

A long list of Balance Adjustments will be available to Global on November 11, 2025 08:00 UTC, about 5 days after the launch of the Unity Cup. A maintenance will be performed first at 02:00 UTC on the same day.

This balance adjustment introduces major changes from the JP 1st Anniversary several months early, such as the Guts rework, new race mechanics, better bad condition management, and mostly bufffed skills and events. Expect the Unity Cup experience to be a bit more different to how it was experienced on other servers previously.
