Trackblazer: Start of the Climax (Make a new track!! ～クライマックス開幕～, Make a new track!! ～Climax Kaimaku～) is a playable scenario in the Umamusume: Pretty Derby mobile game . It was added as the 3rd permanent scenario in the game on February 24th, 2022, coinciding with the game's first anniversary. It was released globally on March 12, 2026. Rivals In graded races (G1-G3), if you have both distance and surface aptitude at C or higher, a rival can appear. This is indicated by the red and blue jagged "VS" bubble and effect. Once you defeated the rival, you will receive a random skill hint related to either race distance and/or strategy used during the race. Win/ Loss conditions are similar to Legend Races: Win if you are first, Draw if neither you nor the rival place first, and Loss if the rival places first. Twinkle Star Climax Twinkle Star Climax banner Twinkle Star Climax is a three-leg final tournament set for the end of the scenario, replacing URA Finale . The goal for this tournament is to gather the most points out of 3 races during the period. Each of the races can occur in one of five variations: sprint, mile, medium, long, and dirt. Similar to URA Finale, the distance and surface are decided by the races you participated in throughout the three years of training. In addition, Junko Hosoe appears as a special color commentator. Each of the Twinkle Star Climax races gives a maximum of 10 Victory Points for the first place, resulting in a maximum of 30 Victory Points for winning all three. Victory Points are indicated by a rainbow-colored star with "Pt" written on it. Additionally, the player also gets +10 to all stats and +30 Skill Pts for each leg of the Twinkle Star Climax they finish. This stat gain scales with Race Bonus effects provided by Hammers and support cards.
