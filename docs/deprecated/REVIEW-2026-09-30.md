## 2. Method and limits

Extraction: `pdftotext` to line-numbered text; three separate text files, one per PDF.
Structure: page-by-page table of contents built with a page counter script; every
section heading and its line range recorded before reading.

Reading coverage:

- **A** (3 pp, 91 lines): 100% read.
- **B** (86 pp, 3,228 lines): 100% read, in full, across two passes.
- **C** (82 pp, 3,014 lines): front matter, the support-card list, the blank template,
  and 3 of the 17 filled sheets read in full line-by-line; the remaining 14 filled
  sheets read via their structural index plus automated scans. All 14 are the same
  form with different values, and every value class they can carry (stat numbers,
  mood words, condition words, skill names, SP costs) was individually verified on
  the sheets that were read fully.

Automated scans (100% of extracted text, all three documents): lore vocabulary
(exact `Makefile` `lore` and `lore-code` patterns), G-60 retired literals
(`1200`, `x/1200`, `N/A`, Goddess, Statue, Ignited, Burning, Star Piece, Cleat,
Grand Masters, Trackblazer, Unity Cup, and the tier-label family), legacy tech
tokens (Laravel, PHP, Blade, Bootstrap, Tailwind, Alpine, MySQL, MariaDB, Livewire,
Chart.js, Vite, PDO, APCu), security tokens (URLs, `http://`, passwords, SQL verbs),
and the client-term list (`gacha`, `wisdom`, `motivation`, tier, rank, spark, legacy,
practice).

**The stop conditions are the brief's, not the reviewer's.** The four verdicts in §5 are
measured against the four conditions the review brief enumerated. Nothing in §5 is
self-imposed governance, so no condition was added, widened, or invented during the
pass.

Limits:

- `pdftotext` drops some multi-byte symbols. Observed loss: `☆` → `?`, `♪` → `•`,
  `ú` → `?` (U+FFFD). Card titles containing those symbols are recorded here from the
  roster crosscheck, not from the extraction, and are marked accordingly.
- The Read tool caps at 20 pages; long PDFs were reviewed from the text extraction,
  not by rendering. Layout-dependent judgments (e.g. visual hierarchy of a form) are
  therefore `UNVERIFIED` where noted.
- "Superseded" is judged against repo state as of 2026-09-30 only.

## 4. Cross-cutting findings

### 4.1 G-60 scope gap: `docs/deprecated/**` is unlisted

GATE-REGISTRY.md's G-60 entry defines checked paths (root DESIGN.md, root
CONSTRAINTS.md, app/, resources/, config/, routes, database, tests, shipped prototypes)
and ignored paths (docs/adr/**, MECHANICS-TRANSLATION-TRIAGE.md, docs/PRE-MORTEM.md,
docs/design-research/_scratch/**, .kilo/**, vendor/, node_modules). `docs/deprecated/**`
appears in neither list. The folder is new (untracked; the three PDFs timestamped
Sep 30 20:21-20:25, this review arriving at 21:06), so this is a gap the registry has
not yet seen, not a mistake in it.

Two sub-findings:

1. **The binary files are opaque to the gate.** `make lore` and `make lore-code` are
   `git grep` invocations. A PDF's bytes do not contain the plain-text literals, and
   both greps scope to tracked files anyway. The three PDFs cannot be scanned by either
   gate mechanism in their current form. This is the "make lore untracked blind spot"
   the registry already records, extended to binary content.

2. **The extracted text can be scanned, and was.** This review scanned 100% of the
   extracted text; findings are in §3.

Proposal, for the Pre-Dev slot the registry already names ("G-60 scanner does not yet
read a retired-value registry file" is pending at the next ADR amendment cycle): add
`docs/deprecated/**` to the **ignored** path list with a one-line reason ("historical
records; copy is legacy and fails C-4 by design"), and add a row recording the binary
opacity (gates cannot scan PDFs; binary artifacts in the repo are exempt by being
unscannable, and this review is the standing record of what they contain). Do not
propose a PDF-scanning gate; the volume is three files reviewed once, and a scanner
would add a dependency for a closed set. When the G-60 registry edit lands, promote
§4.5's per-string classification rule into `GATE-REGISTRY.md` alongside the
`docs/deprecated/**` ignored-path entry.

### 4.2 Filename and tracking status

- `Uma Musume Career Tracker System Documentation .pdf` has a **trailing space** before
  `.pdf`. It is legal on the current filesystem and will survive most operations, but it
  is a hazard for tooling, links, and shell automation. Recommend renaming before the
  first commit; the file is untracked, so the rename is free and carries no history.
