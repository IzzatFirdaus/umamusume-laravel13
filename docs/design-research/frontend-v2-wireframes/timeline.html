  1. Phase grouping is collapsible, which is the corpus's own pattern. DESIGN-CORPUS.md §5 declares "grouping by phase, collapsible — exactly the shape a 24-turn run needs", and this screen is where that shape belongs.
  2. Turn numbers are the spine, left-aligned and monospace. A record is read by finding a turn; putting the number first makes scanning vertical rather than horizontal.
  3. Blank is not zero. Energy and SP columns are empty where the import did not capture them. A zero would claim a reading nobody took.
  4. The finale block sits outside the 72-turn grid and is grouped separately, because the post-December block is not part of the three-year grid the client counts.
  5. Four races, four statuses, one word each. "1st" is the only claim made; no score, no grade, no aggregate, per the app's no-score rule.
  6. Continuity (uilaws) is why this is a rail. Elements arranged along a line are read as related in sequence, which is exactly what a turn log is. Phase grouping is collapsible but the spine never breaks, because a broken rail stops reading as one run.
  7. Heuristic coverage. H1. Every recorded turn is its own entry with its deltas, so a run’s state is reconstructable rather than summarised.
  8. Framework coverage. Gestalt on continuity, and Norman on the gulf of evaluation. The rail never breaks mid-run, and every turn carries its deltas so the record can be read back rather than trusted.
  10. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is at the head of the rail, where DESIGN.md §2.2 puts a control that belongs to a pane: the penalty for a distant control is exactly why the top-bar version was wrong. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
