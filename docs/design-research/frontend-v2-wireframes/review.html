Why this exists

A wrong match silently poisons every downstream screen: a run attributed to the wrong trainee gets the wrong aptitudes, growth row and unique skill. The queue exists so a guess becomes a question.

Annotation key · Review queue

  1. A question, not an error. The queue is a normal part of importing, and the copy says so. Treating an uncertain match as a failure would push Trainers toward accepting whatever the importer guessed.
  2. Confidence is a word and a policy. "Exact" is decisive and pre-selected; "fuzzy" is suggested and never pre-selected. Naming the policy is what stops an over-eager default.
  3. The consequence is stated. The right-hand card explains that a wrong match corrupts every downstream screen, which is why a human is asked at all.
  4. "No match" is a first-class state, shown as a caution pill rather than hidden. Some names genuinely are not in the catalogue.
  5. Zeigarnik Effect is the queue’s justification. Unresolved imports are kept in front of the Trainer precisely because unfinished work is remembered and finished work is not. A queue that resolved silently would leave the uncertain matches forgotten rather than decided.
  6. Heuristic coverage. H9. An uncertain match is framed as a question with the evidence beside it, which is the diagnose-and-recover half of the heuristic.
  7. Framework coverage. Shneiderman 3 on informative feedback and Information architecture 1 on objects. An uncertain match is a question with its evidence beside it, not an error, because it is an object awaiting a decision rather than a fault.
  9. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is at the head of the rail, where DESIGN.md §2.2 puts a control that belongs to a pane: the penalty for a distant control is exactly why the top-bar version was wrong. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
