Annotation key · 500

  1. No stack trace, no framework debug page. The error detail belongs in the log; the screen carries the action and the reassurance.
  2. "Not something you did" is stated. A 500 is the state where a Trainer is most likely to assume they broke something.
  3. Data unchanged, stated. The second sentence answers whether a half-finished turn was written.
  4. Two exits, no retry loop. An automatic retry on a 500 can multiply the failing request; a human decides.
