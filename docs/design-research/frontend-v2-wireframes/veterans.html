Annotation key · Veterans

  1. Three grade columns, not a total. A veteran's value for inheritance is which family carries which grade; collapsing them into one number would hide the only decision a Trainer makes here.
  2. The two Vokda rows are real duplicates. Two separate careers can produce two identical records, and the list says so rather than deduplicating them — they are different runs, not one row shown twice.
  3. Team eligibility is arithmetic the page does for you. Seven registered, three needed, two teams available. That is the Goal-Gradient frame on a library screen with no other goal.
  4. The star ceiling is stated, and it is three on Global. It bounds what a veteran can become. The ★4 cap is the JP-only factor-research item and is named as such rather than printed as if it applied here.
  5. Heuristic coverage. H1. Seven banked veterans and the three needed for a team are both stated, so the library’s state relative to the requirement is visible.
  6. Framework coverage. Gerhardt-Powals 3 on fusing data and Information architecture 5 on front doors. Seven banked against the three a team needs is one fused statement of whether the requirement is met, and the library is fully usable when arrived at directly.
  8. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is at the head of the rail, where DESIGN.md §2.2 puts a control that belongs to a pane: the penalty for a distant control is exactly why the top-bar version was wrong. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
  9. The picture slots are drawings, and absence is a normal state. The app renders a mirrored card_portrait beside each veteran row at 48px (VeteranRow.vue, DESIGN.md §4.7). A file the mirror does not hold renders nothing — no grey box, no placeholder glyph — and the row keeps its name. The dashed frames here mark cells the picture would occupy, and the subject inside each names the picture that belongs there.
