Annotation key · Legacy Lab

  1. Saved legacies, not saved single parents. The unit that matters is the pair, because the two sparks combine. Storing pairs is the structure the inheritance rule implies.
  2. The value table is three rows. Blue spark grades and the pink range are the whole economy of a legacy, and a Trainer comparing pairs decides on them.
  3. Compare is the filled action. Building a legacy is exploration; comparing two is the decision. The primary button sits on the decision.
  4. Law of Uniform Connectedness: the unit is the pair. Two parents are shown together because the sparks combine; a saved legacy is a pair, not two records. Presenting them apart would hide the thing the screen exists to compare.
  5. Heuristic coverage. H6. The saved pairs show their sparks together, so the choice is made from what is visible rather than from a remembered combination.
  6. Framework coverage. Information architecture 1 on objects and Gestalt on common region. A saved pair is one object, shown as one group, because the two sparks combine.
  8. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is in the top bar because the bar is the one row present at every width. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
