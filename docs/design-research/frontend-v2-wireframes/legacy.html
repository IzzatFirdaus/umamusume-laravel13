Annotation key · Legacy Lab

  1. Saved legacies, not saved single parents. The unit that matters is the pair, because the two sparks combine. Storing pairs is the structure the inheritance rule implies.
  2. The value table is three rows. Blue spark grades and the pink range are the whole economy of a legacy, and a Trainer comparing pairs decides on them.
  3. Compare is the filled action. Building a legacy is exploration; comparing two is the decision. The primary button sits on the decision.
  4. Law of Uniform Connectedness: the unit is the pair. Two parents are shown together because the sparks combine; a saved legacy is a pair, not two records. Presenting them apart would hide the thing the screen exists to compare.
