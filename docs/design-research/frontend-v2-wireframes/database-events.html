Retry rules

An event choice is final. The Alarm Clock is the game's retry item and it covers goal races whose placing was missed, and a lost Team Race — not an event.

○ A guide narrows the Alarm Clock further: a player may use up to three per run. The catalogue states the item's own limits rather than implying an unbounded undo.

Annotation key · Events

  1. "Effects documented?" is a column. Most events have no published effect table, and saying so per row is more honest than a catalogue that looks complete.
  2. Scenario scoping is explicit. The secret-event row states it does not fire in Trackblazer, which is a real mechanical difference and not a caveat.
  3. Retry rules sit beside the events. A Trainer looking up an event after a bad outcome is exactly who needs to know what can and cannot be undone.
  4. Law of Proximity puts the caveat on the row it qualifies. "Effect not published" sits in the row it applies to rather than in a footnote, because a qualification that travels away from its value stops being read.
