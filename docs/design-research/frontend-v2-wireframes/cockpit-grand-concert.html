Annotation key · Our Grand Concert cockpit

  1. This is the worst Goal-Gradient case, and the page says why. The other three scenarios own progress data that renders in the wrong place; here the central system stores no number at all, so five honest absences are the correct render.
  2. The loop is explained even though its numbers are missing. Lessons, Songs, the gauge and the concert cadence are published mechanics; a Trainer can plan the run even while the app cannot count Performance.
  3. Payouts are stated per action type. 5 SP per technique Lesson against 25 per Song Lesson is a 5× difference that decides how a Trainer spends Performance.
  4. Heuristic coverage. H1. Performance and Live Performance Expectations are shown as progress towards a named threshold, which is the state a concert decision turns on.
  5. Framework coverage. Shneiderman 4 on closure and Gerhardt-Powals 3 on fusing data. The performance gauge and the concert countdown fuse to one threshold the decision turns on, and the run ends on a stated outcome rather than a gauge.
  7. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is in the top bar because the bar is the one row present at every width. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
