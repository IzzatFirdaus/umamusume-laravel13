Annotation key · Dashboard

  1. One primary action. "Resume career" is the only filled button in the resume card; every other action is an outline or a link. That is one filled action per screen, so the accent means "this is the next click".
  2. The rail is discrete marks, not a bar. At turn 67 of 72 a bar would read as a fraction; seventy-two marks let the eye count what is left. The length is the model's own 72-turn career grid.
  3. Absence is printed, not dropped. Energy and Team Rank keep their tiles and print N/A with the reason in the title, because the controller reports a null rather than a zero.
  4. Counts are the catalogue's. 67 trainees, 106 forms, 1,910 skills and 559 support cards are the counts assets/data/*.js holds; the running app's trainee counter reads 71 because the dev database also holds four test-fixture trainees the catalogue excludes.
  5. Region order matches the app. Active Career, Quick Actions, Recent Veterans and Data Status are the four regions Dashboard.vue composes, in its reading order.
