Effects that decide a deck slot

Friendship BonusMultiplies the tile's payout. Two cards multiply: 1.25 × 1.30 = 1.625.
Initial Friendship GaugeRaises the gauge the card starts with, so it can reach orange sooner.
Specialty PriorityRaises how often the card appears at its preferred facility.
Failure ProtectionShrinks the failure roll on a shared tile.
Wit Friendship RecoveryScales the Energy Wit friendship training returns.

○ Cap-setting effects exist in the vocabulary but are carried by zero of the 559 cards. No UI offers a "cap raised by support cards" line, because the catalogue contains no card that does it.

Annotation key · Support cards

  1. Effect chips are words, not icons. "Initial Friendship Gauge" rather than a symbol, because the client's own label is the only name a Trainer will recognise in game.
  2. Pal cards print "None" for the friendship bonus. The absence is the deciding fact for that type, so it is a labelled cell rather than an empty one. All 296 non-Pal, non-Group SSRs carry the effect; the 11 JP Pal SSRs do not.
  3. Type counts sit on the page. The Global/JP asymmetry is why a deck advice from a JP guide can mislead; showing the counts makes that concrete instead of abstract.
  4. The zero-coverage fact is stated. The cap-setting effect family is real vocabulary with no shipped card. A catalogue that silently omitted it would look complete while missing a documented mechanism; one that offered it would display a feature the data does not contain.
  5. One row per card, effects summarised. The card detail owns the full ladder; the index owns comparison.
  6. Miller’s Law chunks the seven types. Seven is already at the top of the short-term band, so the type table groups by family and the counts are read as one column per server. A flat list of 559 cards would have no chunk boundary at all.
  7. Heuristic coverage. H8. The seven types and the five deciding effects, with the counts that do not decide pushed behind a note.
  8. Framework coverage. Information architecture 6 on multiple classification and 3 on disclosure. The library is browsable by type, by rarity and by server, and the index shows the deciding effects while the rest sits behind a note.
  10. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is in the top bar because the bar is the one row present at every width. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
  12. The way back to the hub is a strip, not a word in a corner. The breadcrumb linked "Database", but it sits in the top bar on the brand-blue chrome with no underline, so it read as a word rather than as a link and a Trainer looking for the way back found the global rail instead. The nine areas are one family, so they are grouped as one family: a sub-navigation strip of the nine, with the current one marked, and the breadcrumb link now visibly a link. Information Architecture principle 7 (focused navigation) and principle 5 (front doors).
  14. The type is a chip, and the chip is never the mark alone. The client identifies a support card by the chip it wears, so the type column carries the client's own glyphs in the client's own chip colours. The word stays beside the mark, because a mark alone breaks WCAG 1.4.1 and because a stat carries no saturated identity hue (DESIGN-CORPUS.md:371-393): the identity is the glyph plus a whisper of band tint, and the hue is spent on meaning.
