Effects that decide a deck slot

Friendship BonusMultiplies the tile's payout. Two cards multiply: 1.25 × 1.30 = 1.625.
Initial Friendship GaugeRaises the gauge the card starts with, so it can reach orange sooner.
Specialty PriorityRaises how often the card appears at its preferred facility.
Failure ProtectionShrinks the failure roll on a shared tile.
Wit Friendship RecoveryScales the Energy Wit friendship training returns.

○ Cap-setting effects exist in the vocabulary but are carried by zero of the 559 cards. No UI offers a "cap raised by support cards" line, because the catalogue contains no card that does it.

Annotation key · Support cards

  1. Effect chips are words, not icons. "Initial Friendship Gauge" rather than a symbol, because the client's own label is the only name a Trainer will recognise in game.
  2. Pal cards print "None" for the friendship bonus. The absence is the deciding fact for that type, so it is a labelled cell rather than an empty one. All 296 non-Pal, non-Group SSRs carry the effect; the 11 JP Pal SSRs do not.
  3. Type counts sit on the page. The Global/JP asymmetry is why a deck advice from a JP guide can mislead; showing the counts makes that concrete instead of abstract.
  4. The zero-coverage fact is stated. The cap-setting effect family is real vocabulary with no shipped card. A catalogue that silently omitted it would look complete while missing a documented mechanism; one that offered it would display a feature the data does not contain.
  5. One row per card, effects summarised. The card detail owns the full ladder; the index owns comparison.
  6. Miller’s Law chunks the seven types. Seven is already at the top of the short-term band, so the type table groups by family and the counts are read as one column per server. A flat list of 559 cards would have no chunk boundary at all.
