Annotation key · Shop items

  1. The rating column is attributed in its header. "Rating (one guide)" is printed, not implied. A column of Must Buy pills without attribution reads as the tool's own advice, which is exactly what the app must not do.
  2. Rules sit beside the catalogue. Unlock, refresh, copy limit, sales, stacking and overwrite are the constraints that decide a purchase, and they belong on the same screen as the prices.
  3. The unconfirmed duration is stated as unconfirmed. The plan carries that claim at badge unknown, so the catalogue does not print a number for it.
  4. Independence of the two currencies is spelled out. It is the most likely arithmetic mistake on this screen, and one sentence prevents it.
  5. Law of Proximity attaches the rating to its source. "Rating (one guide)" is in the column header, not a note below the table, because a one-guide rating read as consensus is the failure the header prevents.
  6. Heuristic coverage. H9. The rating names its source in the header, so a one-guide figure cannot be read as consensus.
