Annotation key · Snapshot review

  1. Three states, three sentences. Known names the number, Unknown says the client shows nothing, and Not provided says the field was left alone. A reviewer can tell "I don't know this" from "I didn't fill it in".
  2. The confirm re-posts what was read back. The button commits the payload this screen displays rather than re-collecting it, so what was read is what is written.
  3. The empty confirmation is stated, not faked. This wireframe has no entered snapshot, so every row prints Not provided and the page says so; inventing numbers here would be the exact error the review exists to prevent.
  4. Back does not lose the entries. The setup form re-hydrates from the parked payload, so a Trainer who returns meets their own entries rather than a blank form.
