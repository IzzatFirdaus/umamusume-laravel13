Annotation key · Inheritance event

  1. The before/after table is the decision. A Trainer picks between sparks by their cap consequence, so the consequence is printed. Picking a ★3 blue and then finding out it moved a cap is the opposite of a decision surface.
  2. Blue, pink and white are named, not colour-coded only. The spark family is the word (Blue = stat, Pink = aptitude, White = skill hint); the colour reinforces. A colour-blind Trainer reads the same table. Blue's +21 stat and its +16 cap are printed as two labelled effects, because one moves the stat and the other moves its ceiling.
  3. One pick per parent, enforced by radios. The moment grants one spark per parent; allowing two would promise a rule the game does not have.
  4. Inheritance adds to the entered ceiling, not the published one. This run already carries entered ceilings above the scenario's 1300, and the note says so. Collapsing the two is the same defect the cockpit audit filed as A3.
  5. "Skip this moment" is offered. Inheritance is optional at every one of its three moments; a screen that only offers a pick implies the moment is mandatory.
  6. Law of Uniform Connectedness: the inheritance is drawn, not listed. Six ancestors read as one line of descent only if the connection between generations is visible, so the rule between columns carries it. Six unconnected boxes would be six records, not a family (lawsofux.com/law-of-uniform-connectedness, and Continuity on uilaws.com).
  7. Empty grandparent slots read as empty, not as absent. A dashed border and the words "slot open" say the slot exists and is unfilled. Hiding it would misstate how many ancestors the rule provides.
  8. Heuristic coverage. H6. The parent slots and each spark’s carried effect are on screen at the moment of picking, so the choice is made by recognition.
  9. Framework coverage. Gestalt on continuity and common region, and Information architecture 1 on objects. The six ancestors read as one line of descent because the connection between generations is drawn, and each spark is an object with a family, a grade and a carried effect.
  11. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is at the head of the rail, where DESIGN.md §2.2 puts a control that belongs to a pane: the penalty for a distant control is exactly why the top-bar version was wrong. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
