Annotation key · Legacy comparison

  1. Differences are a column, not a colour. "same" and "+24 to A" say what changed; the row tint only draws the eye to the rows that carry a difference.
  2. Identical rows still print. A comparison that hides matching rows makes a Trainer wonder whether the property was checked at all.
  3. No aggregate winner. The page states the cap gap and lets the Trainer decide; scoring two legacies would be the invented recommendation the app forbids.
  4. Contrast carries the difference, and the word carries it too. "same" and "+24 to A" are a column rather than a tint, because a row tint alone would be colour carrying meaning without a word.
  5. Heuristic coverage. H9. Differences are a worded column and the tint only reinforces it, which is H9’s rule that state is never colour alone.
  6. Framework coverage. WCAG 1.4.1 on use of colour, and Information architecture 6 on multiple classification. The difference is a worded column and the tint only reinforces it, and the two ancestors are browsable by more than one axis.
  8. The rail collapses, and the word never leaves the DOM. The app can collapse its global navigation to marks instead of words, and this set does the same (AppLayout.vue:191-233). A collapsed destination moves its label to sr-only and gains a title, so it keeps its accessible name; the active one keeps aria-current="page" and a left rule, so state never rides on colour alone. The control is in the top bar because the bar is the one row present at every width. The choice is localStorage, not a Preference row, because Preference::KEYS is exactly theme and failure_estimate.
  10. The type is a chip, and the chip is never the mark alone. The client identifies a support card by the chip it wears, so the type column carries the client's own glyphs in the client's own chip colours. The word stays beside the mark, because a mark alone breaks WCAG 1.4.1 and because a stat carries no saturated identity hue (DESIGN-CORPUS.md:371-393): the identity is the glyph plus a whisper of band tint, and the hue is spent on meaning.
