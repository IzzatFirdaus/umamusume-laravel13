Annotation key · Race planner

  1. Race facts only. Every figure is a race-catalogue column, a value the Trainer entered, or a named absence. The win probability and expected risk are held on ADR-0016 and print N/A with the reason.
  2. The deadline states the next obligation, not a promise. It names the next race the run has not cleared and says plainly that the tool does not predict whether the rest are kept.
  3. Comparison is a table, not two card grids. Identical properties align vertically so the Trainer reads one difference per row instead of holding two cards in their head.
  4. Races the trainee's aptitude forbids are shown with the refusal. A greyed control that names why beats a row that silently disappears.
  5. Race names come from the scenario's catalogue. Turns and fans gates are not in this wireframe's dataset, so each prints N/A with its reason rather than an illustrative number.
