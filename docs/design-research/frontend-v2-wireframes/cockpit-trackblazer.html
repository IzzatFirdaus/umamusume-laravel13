Annotation key · Trackblazer cockpit

  1. This is the scenario where the app already owns a real target. 60 / 300 / 300 are published checkpoints, so the bar above has a genuine denominator — unlike Unity Cup, where the run holds no targets at all.
  2. "88 short" is the whole page in two words. The runway is the decision; everything else on the screen supports it.
  3. Fatigue is shown as the row for the current chain. Two races in a row carries no penalty; the third is where Mood Down jumps to 60%. Printing the whole grid lets a Trainer plan the run, not just the turn.
  4. The two currencies are separated in one sentence. It is the most likely arithmetic mistake on this scenario.
  5. Goal-Gradient is the whole panel. 212 of 300, 88 short, 22 turns left. The law is about distance to the goal rather than the current value, so the shortfall and the turn count sit beside the bar; a bare total would answer a question nobody asked.
  6. Heuristic coverage. H1. Grade Points against the next deadline, plus the coin balance and the rotation countdown, so every live resource has a visible state.
