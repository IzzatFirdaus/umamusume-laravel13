Annotation key · Training decision

  1. Five disciplines, one row each, gains right-aligned in monospace. Comparable numbers in a column is Fitts and Miller working together: the eye compares five gain figures in one vertical scan instead of five paragraphs.
  2. Energy is signed and coloured, and the sign carries the meaning. "−19 Energy" is a loss (blue, per DESIGN.md: down is blue, never red); "+5 Energy" on Wit is a gain (orange). The word and the sign inform; colour reinforces.
  3. Wit is marked "no Energy" with a tag, not just a plus sign. It is the one discipline that refunds, and the plan makes it the low-risk filler when resting is not wanted.
  4. The recommendation is chosen by default but not hidden. Rest is preselected and marked, yet the five disciplines stay fully selectable: the advisor advises, it does not act (design-2.0 §2, player agency).
  5. The outcome fields are separate from the choice. Choosing a discipline and recording what the client showed are two facts; merging them would invite the tool to invent a reading.
  6. The career nav is greyed to a stepper, not repeated as a second tab bar. Cockpit already owns the top-level nav; inside a run, moving between decision screens is a step, not a destination change.
  7. Heuristic coverage. H5 + H7. Both branches of every gain are stated before the commit, and the flow is operable from the keyboard with 1 to 5 selecting and Enter confirming.
