Payouts and pacing C4

  • 5 Skill Points per technique Lesson and 25 per Song Lesson taken since the previous live, plus a +50 Performance cap rise per live.
  • Friendship training pays twice: a friendship session grants two Performance types where ordinary training grants one.
  • Facility → type mapping is image-derived and recorded as such in the master: Speed Dance/Visual, Stamina Passion/Vocal, Power Vocal/Mental, Guts Visual/Dance, Wit Mental/Passion. Not printed as a rule.
  • Song unlock pacing resets every live: before the first live 1-2-3 then 4-4-2-2; before the second, third and fourth 2-2-2 then 4-5-2-2.
  • Make Debut! is granted automatically; GIRLS' LEGEND U belongs to the finale.

○ The plan records four Promo Lives in one place and "four Promo Concerts and one Grand Concert" in notice 905, which is five concerts; the notice is tier S and the plan is the summariser, so the notice's shape is what a UI should print.

Annotation key · Our Grand Concert

  1. C1 — Goal-Gradient fails hardest here, and for a structural reason. The four scenarios fail Goal-Gradient in a graded way: Trackblazer has real targets, URA Finale has real targets that render in the wrong region, Unity Cup has a band system but no career goal, and Our Grand Concert has no number at all. Its one central system, Performance across five types, is explicitly not a panel and stores no value. The honest wireframe draws five empty cells, which is the finding.
  2. C2 — the scenario's vocabulary is far ahead of its surfaces. Promo Concert, Grand Concert, lessons, songs, concert techniques, Live Performance Expectations and Great Success are all first-class in the config and the enum, and none composes a panel. A Trainer on this scenario gets a cockpit that looks like a bare URA run plus one empty Performance strip. That is not a styling gap; it is the same "no number exists" problem as C1, repeated seven times.
  3. C3 — the deliberate panel asymmetry. career_goals: true is set while every other panel is off, so this scenario draws the goals ladder and the baseline strip. That combination is the one grand-concert-panel.spec.ts:49 pins as correct, and it is why ScenarioPanel's gate reads careerGoalsOn || onPanels.length === 0 rather than onPanels.length === 0. Not a defect — but it means two cap-like statements can appear in one region.
  4. Vocabulary is single-sourced. The five Performance types come from App\Enums\PerformanceType and are not repeated in config/scenarios.php. That is a Prägnanz pass: one vocabulary, one owner, so the screen cannot drift from notice 905.
  5. C4 — the loop has a real shape: training pays Performance, Lessons spend it on Songs and techniques, Songs fill the gauge, the gauge sets Great Success, and each live pays out. The panel above now carries that chain with its published figures — 5 SP per technique Lesson, 25 per Song Lesson, +50 Performance cap per live, three Songs to fill the gauge, Lessons opening on turn five, four Promo Concerts plus the Grand Concert on a six-month cadence from late December of Junior Year. None of it is stored per run, so the Cockpit can print the mechanism but not this run's position in it.
  6. C5 — the input lands at turn entry, not on the Cockpit. Our-Grand-Concert-plan.md Slice 6 and its 2026-10-21 dated note: the committing surface is resources/js/pages/Career/TrainingDetail.vue (SCR-CAR-012), which carries the performance[type] / performance[delta] pair. Runs/Show.vue no longer exists — the record screen was retired at the F2 cutover. So the Cockpit's job is a read, and the empty five cells above are correct until a read-side panel exists.
  7. C6 — validation vocabulary, which a reader-facing panel must not echo. The plan rejects Mental, Vocal, Visual, Dancing and dance as invalid terminology; the client prints Composure, Vocals and Visuals. The list is a guard, not a claim that these words may be used. Zero and decimal Performance values are rejected.
  8. C7 — the Speed cap here is the highest in the game on Global. Caps are 1600 / 1300 / 1300 / 1500 / 1300 over a 1200 base, and 1600 Speed is the highest [Global] Speed ceiling in play. The panel marks Speed +400 and Guts +300 because those two carry the scenario's identity in numbers.
