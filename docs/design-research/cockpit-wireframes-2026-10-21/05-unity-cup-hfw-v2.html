Annotation key · what changed, and what the prototype got wrong about this build

Domain facts added from the scenario references (2026-10-21)

  1. 13 — Mood is a multiplier the page should show as one. The client's Mood Effect panel prints five tiers with two effect columns: GREAT +20% training / +4% pre-race, GOOD +10% / +2%, NORMAL 0%, BAD −10% / −2%, AWFUL −20% / −4% (UMAMUSUME_REFERENCE.md §1.1.6). The as-built prints the bare string "GREAT", so a Trainer cannot see that the next session is worth a fifth more. Source is the client panel, marked HIGH-not-corroborated — two JP guides print ±10% instead, and the document records the conflict rather than resolving it in the guides' favour.
  2. 14 — The Team Board is the scenario's permanent fixture, and its four sections are the ones the plan names. Unity-Cup-plan.md §8a makes it a continuous summary rather than a pre-race screen: overall team rank, facility progression (rank → level), team stat ranks (five letters), and race coverage (Sprint / Mile / Medium / Long / Dirt, 3 fielded each). This run records team name, motto, league placement 8, preseason wins 4, 55 team trainings, and burst counts 6 normal + 5 Extreme. It records no team stat ranks, which is why every facility level is unavailable.
  3. 15 — Rank → facility mapping, and the trap it sets. G/F = Lv 1, D/E = Lv 2, B/C = Lv 3, A = Lv 4, S = Lv 5. B and C share a level, so "Stamina C → B" is a rank move that crosses no level boundary. The plan's own dated note (2026-10-21) records this being misdrawn as "C → Lv 2" in an earlier wireframe and corrected. Any UI that prints a rank→level arrow must show the level, not only the letter.
  4. 16 — Advisor priority, which the page currently inverts. Unity-Cup-plan.md §9 ranks: (1) don't end the run, (2) don't wreck the run, (3) burst breakpoints at 4 / 7 / 10 / 13, (4) facility levels derived from team ranks, (5) training target, (6) optimization. The recommendation must also carry a dual readout: trainee gain this turn versus team gain or future opportunity cost. The page today gives the trainee-side reason only.
  5. 17 — Burst band boundaries are a real decision boundary. This run sits at 11 bursts, inside the 10–12 band, with the next boundary at 13. The plan's bands are 4 / 7 / 10 / 13; config's spirit_burst_bands are 4–6, 7–9, 10–12, 13+. One burst short of 13 changes the reward tier, which is exactly the kind of proximity the old Goal-Gradient failure hid.

The 12 changes, applied

  1. Two nav levels. Career nav is a 13px tab bar with a 3px accent underline; the section nav demotes to a 12.5px strip carrying a "Cockpit" level label. They were two visually identical text rows 100px apart.
  2. One filled action, with its reason. "Rest this turn" is the page's only --primary-action fill, in the identity bar, with "Energy is in the low band" attached. The as-built renders it as an outlined chip among five key-value pairs.
  3. The lime is gone. Nine saturated bars became zero. Stat bars use --ink-faint; section headings use a 2px --rule underline. The only saturated colours left are: the Active pill (--status-positive), the one action (--primary-action), and --risk where a value is genuinely wrong.
  4. Absent rows dropped, actionable absences promoted. "Team rank: not recorded" stays as a value in the grid; the paragraphs explaining why are gone. Nothing was silently deleted — absence still prints.
  5. One field grid, one left edge. Three fieldsets (Identity / Rarity & growth / Stat ceilings) on one grid, all at x=0 of the card. The as-built puts Status/Scenario at x=130 and Rarity/ceilings at x=515.
  6. One save model. One sticky bar, one verb "Save changes", "Discard" present only when dirty. The as-built carries seven verbs in four button styles.
  7. Units inside the field. The % sits inside the input box, right-aligned; the scenario ceiling is a one-line caption under the field instead of wrapping to two.
  8. Red carries a word. Stamina 1322 shows "over scenario 1300" in --risk. The colour reinforces; the words inform.
  9. Words, not glyphs. set by you / auto replaces "Σ Calculated".
  10. File paths out of the UI. Every §-and-line-number citation becomes one "Source" link to the app's existing data-sources page.
  11. Progress is a progress element. Applied where a denominator exists (the bracket). Not applied to obligations, because "1 of 8" does not describe this run's data — three URA Finals rows are undated unknown, so a bar would imply precision the data lacks.
  12. Advanced/destructive separated. Correction and delete live in a collapsed "Advanced" disclosure after the routine actions, not above them.

Still open, not settled by this wireframe

  2. Reaching four semantic colours means retiring or repurposing roles — green-tint, green-line, pick, pick-line, the six band tints, nine grade fills. That is a token migration whose acceptance test is DesignTokensTest.php's count, not a design change.
  3. The "Edit identity ▸ while open" defect is visible in the prototype at line 366. Cockpit.vue:617 has <details> with no open attribute, so the default-closed case is correct; whether an open form renders a collapsed triangle needs a live render to confirm, and this host cannot launch a browser.
  4. The empty-state rule as applied here drops explanation paragraphs but keeps every absence as a value. Whether that is the intended reading of "if the value is absent, drop the row" needs the owner's confirmation — dropping the row outright would break the repo's standing rule that an absence prints with its reason.
