Epithet routes

Rival races appear on their own schedule: randomly, starting Early August of Junior Year. No fixed plan can hold one.

A race may carry a rival; beating one grants a skill hint tied to the race distance or running style, requiring C aptitude or better in that distance. An epithet needs every race in its route won, so a skipped race can break a route (Trackblazer-plan.md §9 rule 3).

Shop rules B6

Used almost every turn, so the plan puts it in the cockpit as a drawer rather than a separate page.

  • Locked until the Debut race; refreshes every 6 turns; max 5 copies per item.
  • Sales are 10% to 20%.
  • Multi-turn items cannot be used while still active.
  • A lower-grade item used before a higher one is wasted; the reverse is not possible.
  • Race-triggered items last 3 turns — unconfirmed (Trackblazer-plan.md §11 carries it at badge unknown).

○Coins scale with placement, 100 for first and less below, so losing reduces income: race consistency carries a direct economic penalty.

Annotation key · Trackblazer

  1. B1 — this scenario passes Goal-Gradient, and is the model the other three should copy. grade_objectives publishes real checkpoints (60 / 300 / 300 standard; 30 / 200 / 300 dirt-leaning; 60 / 200 / 300 limited turf), so a bar with a target and a remaining figure is honest here — unlike Unity Cup, where the run has no targets at all. The aptitude-conditional split is the Prägnanz win: the right target for the trainee's own surface instead of one generic track.
  2. B2 — Hick's Law / Choice Overload risk. 19 shop items, 12 of them rated "Must Buy" by one guide, with no sort or filter. The config is explicit that the tool recommends nothing ("no row is recommended"), which is honest — but a Must-Buy column is functionally a shortlist, and nineteen rows is a scan, not a decision. Group by role and surface the two or three that answer the current checkpoint.
  3. B3 — Similarity (7) hazard. shop_coins_by_placement (100 / 60 / 60 / 30 / 30 / 0) and grade_point_placement_modifiers (100 / 60 / 40 / 20 / 20 / 10) are two placement tables for two currencies, printed in the same region with near-identical shape. They derive differently and a Trainer will read one as the other.
  4. B4 — Goal-Gradient in the strip. The Grade Point checkpoint belongs in the summary strip, beside the coins that buy the items that pay for it. Today it is a scenario panel below the decision.
  5. B5 — Race Fatigue is a Trackblazer-specific panel the page does not have. The plan puts it above the advisor because the scenario has no race goals, so racing is the economy, and an unbroken chain of races is the main way a run goes wrong. The table is published; this page should render the current chain count against it, not the whole grid. Two conventions stay unverified and must be flagged rather than resolved: whether "3 races" counts the upcoming race, and which year "after late December" means.
  6. B6 — Shop rules are constraints, not decoration. Locked until Debut, refreshes every 6 turns, 5 copies per item, sales 10–20%, multi-turn items cannot stack while active, and a lower-grade item burned before a higher one is wasted. A drawer that does not state these invites the exact mistake the rules prevent. The "race-triggered items last 3 turns" line is unconfirmed and carries its badge.
  7. B7 — Correction: the two currencies are not interchangeable, and the plan says so explicitly. Trackblazer-plan.md §8 records that Grade Points and Shop Coins are "two independent counters and a planner must not derive one from the other", and that config/scenarios.php keeps unity_cup.grade_points = null and unity_cup.shop_coins = null — both Trackblazer-only. Any UI that prints one from the other is wrong. This is the same finding as B3, stated from the plan's side.
  8. B8 — Surplus Grade Points do not carry over, and the plan asks the page to say so. After a period's threshold is met, further points do not help that checkpoint, so the advisor should print "checkpoint met; a race now pays coins, stats and epithet progress only." The wireframe's "88 to go" is only correct because the checkpoint is not yet met.
