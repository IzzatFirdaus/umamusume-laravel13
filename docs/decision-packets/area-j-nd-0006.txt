## Owner Question (Exact)
**Should the owner keep the `not_reached` vocabulary with disclosure (Option A), or attempt to derive `missed` from recorded position (Option B)?**

**Option A**: Keep `not_reached` vocabulary with disclosure
- **Evidence required**: Review claim refuted per ND-6(a); no new state derivation
- **Development blocked**: None — vocabulary and disclosure shipped
- **Release relevance**: Maintains current state; no new functionality

**Option B**: Derive `missed` from recorded position

- **Development blocked**: Schema does not support missed/unrecorded derivation today; would require schema change
