# Decision Packet — House Rule: TOG-11

## Rule Identifier
**Code**: TOG-11

## Rule Context
**Repository House Rule**: The eleventh touch-orbit guideline governing learnability vs. usability balance in interface design.

**Source**: `tognazzini-first-principles-2026-10-21.md`; `UI-UX-PRODUCT-AUDIT-2026-11-18.md`; `framework-ui-ux/usability-heuristics-2026-10-21.md`

## Rule Statement
**TOG-11: Learnability vs. usability balance** — Interface design must balance learnability (ease of first-time use) with usability (efficiency for repeated use). Leaning too far toward one at the expense of the other violates TOG-11. Designs that optimize solely for first-time learnability at the cost of expert efficiency, or vice versa, are non-compliant.

**Rationale**: Both learnability and usability are core design goals. The house rule establishes that neither may be sacrificed entirely for the other.
