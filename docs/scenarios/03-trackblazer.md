In the Japanese version, this scenario is called **Make a New Track (MANT)**, and it was JP's **1st Anniversary scenario**, originally released February 24, 2022. Global's pacing has it arriving well after Unity Cup, giving Unity Cup roughly 4 months as the "current best" training scenario before Trackblazer takes over that role — this mirrors JP's own release cadence, just compressed for Global.

## What Makes Trackblazer Different: No Race Goals

This is Trackblazer's headline mechanical difference from both prior scenarios:

- **URA Finale and Unity Cup** both lock you into a **fixed schedule of mandatory race goals** — miss enough of them, and your career run ends early. Race timing is largely dictated to you.
- **Trackblazer removes this entirely.** Instead of race goals, you're tasked with accumulating **Grade Points** before a series of deadlines. You need to hit Grade Point thresholds by certain points in the career to keep progressing, but *how* you earn those points — which races, how many, in what order — is up to you.
- Because there's no fixed race schedule, you also aren't forced into a specific ranking requirement to "pass" a scripted race the way you are in URA Finale/Unity Cup — the pass condition is about total Grade Points accumulated, not clearing one particular gated event.

**Practical implication:** Trackblazer is the most *player-directed* of the three scenarios. You can race far more freely, chase whichever distances/conditions you want, and effectively self-pace your season — as opposed to URA Finale/Unity Cup's "the calendar tells you what to do next" structure.

## Easier Trophy Collection

Because Trackblazer has fewer structural restrictions than URA Finale or Unity Cup (no locked race calendar, no team-rank gating), it's noted as being **easier to collect race trophies** in general — since you're not constrained to a narrow scripted set of races to try for them.

## Fan Farming Potential

Since you can race as often as you like without a fixed schedule dictating turn usage, Trackblazer makes it **easy to accumulate large numbers of Fans** over a run — especially when paired with Support Cards that carry a **Fan Bonus** effect. If your goal for a specific run is maximizing Fans (for Fan Tier / final grade purposes, or just for flavor), Trackblazer will likely be the most efficient scenario to do that in, precisely because of its race-frequency freedom.

## New Mechanic: Shop Coins & the Trackblazer Shop

- Every race you enter grants **Shop Coins**, with the full amount (100 Coins) awarded for **1st place finishes**; placing lower than 1st grants fewer coins.
- Shop Coins are spent in the **Trackblazer Shop** on items that boost your current run — effects range from direct stat increases, to setting your condition, to restoring Energy, to boosting Support Card bond levels, and more.
- This effectively gives Trackblazer its own **soft in-run economy**, similar in spirit to Unity Cup's Team Rank system, but built around *racing frequently and winning* rather than team training. The more you race and place well, the more you can shop for run-boosting items — creating a direct feedback loop between "race a lot" (which the scenario already encourages via no fixed schedule) and getting stronger for the races still to come.

## New Mechanic: Rival Races & Skill Hints

- Certain races are flagged with a **VS icon**, indicating a **Rival** is present in that race.
- **Beat the Rival outright (get 1st place)** → you receive a random skill hint tied to either the race's distance or the running style you used in that race.
- **Beat the Rival's placement but don't get 1st place yourself** (i.e., you outplace them but someone else wins) → counts as a **draw** with the Rival — no skill hint is granted in this case.
- This means Rival races are best treated as **must-win opportunities** if you want the skill hint reward, not just "beat the specific competitor and coast."

## Secret Events Do Not Trigger

- Umamusume with **secret character events do not get them in Trackblazer.** The example given is **Silence Suzuka**, who normally can unlock a unique "Runaway" running style through her secret event in other scenarios — this is **not obtainable in Trackblazer**, since the secret-event trigger conditions simply don't fire in this scenario.
- **Practical takeaway:** if a specific trainee's build depends on a secret-event-locked outcome (a unique running style, a special skill, etc.), **do not run that trainee in Trackblazer if you need that secret outcome** — pick URA Finale or Unity Cup for that specific career instead, and save Trackblazer runs for trainees whose builds don't depend on secret events, or for pure Fan/trophy farming.

## Best Strategy Summary for Trackblazer (Provisional)

Based on what's confirmed so far:

1. **Race often and race to win** — since Shop Coins scale with placement (100 for 1st place) and there's no fixed schedule punishing you for extra races, frequent racing is both safe and actively rewarded here, unlike in Unity Cup where extra racing costs you Team-building turns.
2. **Prioritize Rival (VS) races and aim for outright 1st place**, not just beating the rival's position, since only a true win grants the skill hint.
3. **Use Fan Bonus support cards** if your goal for the run is fan farming — Trackblazer's race-frequency freedom is the best environment for this among the three scenarios.
4. **Don't run trainees whose build depends on a secret event** in Trackblazer if you need that specific unlock (e.g. Silence Suzuka's Runaway style) — those events won't trigger here.
5. **Spend Shop Coins deliberately** rather than hoarding — since the shop is meant to directly power up your current run, unspent coins at the end of a career provide no benefit.
6. Keep an eye on **official patch notes at Global launch (mid-March 2026)**, given Unity Cup's precedent of receiving significant Global-only mechanical changes shortly after release.
